Executive Summary

Your public sector RFx portfolio tells a clear story: you win big when you compete, but you lose volume. Your average win ($252K) is more than double your average loss ($118K), meaning you're strong on strategic, high-value opportunities but hemorrhaging on smaller deals where price sensitivity dominates. The data reveals $12.6M in lost TCV that is cyclically recoverable — these are government contracts with known rebid cycles, and many are entering their rebid windows now or within the next 18 months.

Three systemic patterns emerge: (1) 30 deals worth $3.3M were lost to "No Decision / Project Delayed" — these are unfinished business that will resurface, (2) 24 price losses ($7.1M TCV) are approaching 3-year contract expirations where incumbents may have underdelivered, and (3) Adobe is the #1 competitor in losses (34%) but you still beat them regularly, suggesting winnable re-engagements.

Strategy D: Public Data Intelligence Plays

Public sector RFPs are public record. Use freely available data to get ahead of the cycle.

SAM.gov Contract Expiration Monitoring: Set up daily alerts for keywords: "electronic signature," "eSignature," "contract lifecycle," "CLM," "document management," "digital signature" across Sources Sought, Pre-Solicitation, and Solicitation notices. Focus on agencies where you have prior touchpoints.
State Procurement Portal Tracking: Monitor state eProcurement portals (BidNet, DemandStar, PublicPurchase) for the ~15 states where you have existing wins or high-value losses. Many states post procurement forecasts 6–12 months before solicitation.
Budget Appropriation Signals: Track state legislature budget sessions (typically Jan–Jun). When a state appropriates money for "IT modernization," "digital transformation," or "paperless government," the eSign/CLM RFP is 6–18 months behind. Cross-reference with your delayed deal list.
AI/Modernization Executive Orders: As of 2025–2026, numerous federal and state governments have issued AI and digital government modernization mandates. These create budget pressure to modernize contract and signature workflows. Frame your outreach around compliance with these mandates.
FOIA/Open Records Requests: For high-value losses (US Courts $2.34M, FL DCF $227K, IL SOS $120K), file FOIA requests for the winning proposals' pricing and evaluation scores. This is legal for public sector contracts and gives you the exact intelligence to adjust your next bid.

Strategy E: "Never Compete Blind Again"

Your 0% win rate on blind RFx demands a structural change in approach.

Pre-RFP Engagement Protocol: For every target agency, engage 6–12 months before the anticipated RFP. Offer free "Digital Transformation Readiness Assessments" to build relationships and shape requirements before the solicitation is written.
RFI Response as Lead Gen: Your win rate on RFIs (28%) is lower than RFPs (27%) but RFIs are pre-purchase signals. Treat every government RFI as a prospecting trigger, not a one-time response. Follow up quarterly.
Partner Leverage: Carahsoft is your primary government channel. 50+ deals flow through them. Invest in joint pipeline reviews, co-hosted government webinars, and shared intelligence on upcoming solicitations. They see the RFPs before you do.

2You cannot win blind. 0% win rate on blind RFx. Every government deal you've ever won involved prior engagement. Invest in pre-RFP relationship building, not RFP response capacity.
3Price losses come back. $7.1M in price losses are on 3–5 year contract clocks. The 2023 losses start expiring in 2026. Build a watch list and engage 12 months before estimated rebid.
